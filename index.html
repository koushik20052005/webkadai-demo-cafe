Our Story

A coffee house that refuses to hurry

Kapi Kottai began in 2019 as a six-stool filter coffee counter. We still brew the way Madras always has — brass filters, first-degree decoction, a full meter of pour — but we also chase what specialty coffee does best: traceable estates, honest roast dates, beans ground seconds before water touches them.

Come for a quick davara before work, or take the slow bar stool and watch a pour-over bloom. Either way, nobody will rush you out.

The Craft

Four steps to your cup

01

Pick your estate

Peaberry from Chikmagalur, washed arabica from Coorg, or a Yercaud natural. The board lists roast dates, always within 21 days.

02

Ground to order

Weighed to the gram and ground seconds before brewing — never pre-ground, never stale. The aroma hits you before the cup does.

03

Slow drip, brass filter

Our filter coffee drips the traditional way, one drop at a time. Espresso and pour-overs get the same patience at the slow bar.

04

The meter pour

Davara to tumbler, a full meter of arc, till the froth stands in soft peaks. That's not showmanship — that's aeration.
